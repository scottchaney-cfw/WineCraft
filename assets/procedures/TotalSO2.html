Total SO2 Procedure

  • Pipette 25mL of wine sample
  • Add 25mL NaOH 1N
  • Place parafilm/cover over flask and wait 10min
  • Add 5mL starch indicator solution
  • Add 10mL 25% H2SO4
  • Titrate wiht Iodine/Potassium Iodate .02N to dark purple
  • mL titrant * 23.36 = x ppm of total SO2
  • Total should be less than 100

mL Iodine * N Iodine * 32 * 1000 / mL wine sample

Strandardization of Iodine is against a sodium thiosulfate standard

N Iodine = mL thiosulfate * N thiosulfate / mL iodine solution

10mL thiosulfate is common
