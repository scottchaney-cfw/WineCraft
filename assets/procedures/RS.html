Residual Sugar

Standardization of dextrose solution

  • Run a blank sample of DI water to standardize the dextrose solution.
  • Add 20mL of DI water to the 250mL flask.
  • Add 25mL with pipette (using bulb suction) of equal parts solution of Fehling’s A & B to the flask.
  • Heat the flask while stirring. Add several drops of methyl blue to flask.
  • Fill burette with dextrose solution and zero out.
  • Once the solution begins to boil begin adding the dextrose solution.
  • Record the point at which the color change from blue to rust brown occurs.

Running of Sample

  • Riesling wine will be used in this scenario.
  • Pipette 10mL of wine sample into 100mL volumetric flask. Fill to volume with DI water.
  • This provides a 1:10 reduction of sugar concentration for the test to read accurately below 2.5% RS.
  • Pipette 20mL of the solution into the Erlenmeyer flask.
  • Add 25mL of equal parts Fehling’s A&B solution to the flask.
  • Heat the flask while stirring. Add several drops of methyl blue to flask.
  • Fill burette with dextrose solution and zero out.
  • Once the solution begins to boil begin adding the dextrose solution.
  • Record the point at which the color change from blue to rust brown occurs.

Formulation

RS (g/L) = (B-W)(0.005 g/mL)(1000 mL/L) / sample volume (mL)

RS (g/L) = (B-W)/4 -for a 20mL sample
