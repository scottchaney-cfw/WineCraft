pH Meter Calibration

  • Press On button.
  • Fill small beaker with 7 buffer.
  • Fill small beaker with 4 buffer.
  • Unscrew cap on probe cover, slide cap up probe.
  • Place probe and temp probe in 7 buffer. (Bring buffer to 25C if temp probe does not fit.)
  • Press Calibrate button.
  • When hour glass disappears and CFM is flashing press the CFM button, take probe out, rinse with DI water and shake.
  • Place probe in 4 buffer.
  • When hour glass disappears and CFM is flashing press the CFM button, take probe out, rinse with DI water and shake.

Press measure button to verify curve fit is 96 or higher.
