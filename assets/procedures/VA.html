VA Procedure

Uses Cash Still

  • Add de-ionized water to outer chamber. The water in the outer chamber needs to be about the heating coils. (Turn knob, tape is in up position for outer chamber)
  • Water source is only turned out a little.
  • Turn on coil to boil water
  • Dilute 30% H2O2 down to .3%. (Use gloves)
  • Teflon gel added to all glass ground connections for air tight fit.
  • Residual sugar wines require 1-2 drops of anti-foam to keep test accurate
  • Collect 10mL of wine in beaker, de-gas if freshly fermented for 3-5min via vacuum (not too much as the vacuum could pull out the VA vapors)
  • Add .5mL of .3% H2O2 to beaker to remove/neutralize any SO2 in the sample that would throw off VA measure.
  • Turn knob tape to up position for inner chamber.
  • Add sample.
  • Add de-ionized water to wash down any residual wine in the tube and to allow 100mL of sample to collect.
  • Close the knob, collect 100mL of distillate.
  • Titrate sample with .1N NaOH.

To clean Cash Still

  • Open knob valve, turn off coils.
  • Rinse out system while still hot
  • Turn off water source.

*- between tests, turn coils back on prior to adding new sample so it does not vacuum up into outer chamber.
