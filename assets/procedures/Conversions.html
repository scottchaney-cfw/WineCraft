Conversions

Sulfite Additions

  • Tons of grapes measured prior to crush
  • Depending on the condition of the grapes more or less ppm KMBS (Potassium Metabisulfate) will be desired.
  • Formula:
  • grams of KMBS = Tons * Est Gallons Per Ton * Desired free ppm SO2 * 0.00757

Gallons of juice or wine

  • Recommended ppm is based on impiracle data for .8g/L and .5g/L
  • .8g/L corresponds to the 40ppm free SO2 rule of thumb for wine at 3.5pH
  • .5g/L corresponds to the lighter 25ppm free SO2 trend of reduced sulphites
  • Formula:
  • grams of KMBS = Gallons * (Recommended ppm - Measured ppm) * 0.00757

Note that .8g/L corresponds to the 40ppm free rule of thumb for wine at 3.5pH.

.5g/L corresponds to the lighter 25ppm trend of reduced sulfites. Whites, especially sweets are kept at .8g/L, reds can be kept at .5g/L

  • The empirical determination of ppm recommended at a given pH can be found by:
  • .8g/L ppm = 0.0149 * e(2.2573 * pH)
  • .5g/L ppm = 0.0093 * e(2.2535 * pH)
  • The consolidated factor is obtained from:
  • 1 ppm = 1mg/L
  • The percentage molecular weight of SO2 dissociated from KMBS is 57.6%
  • (1mg/L) * ( 1g/1000mg) * 3.78541 L/gal * 1 gal * 1/0.576
  • 3.78541 / (1000 * 0.576) = 0.00657
  • In order to account for storage degradation, impurities, and initial binding during addition the factor is increased 15% to 0.00757

Simple Conversions

  • lbs * 2000 = Tons
  • Gallons = 3.71845 * Liters
  • lbs = 2.21 * kg
  • oz = 28.3495 * g
  • tsp = 4.92892 * mL

YAN Additions

  • Recommended YAN is based on yeast strain requirements and nutrient used.
  • New nutrients and yeast strains may be added through Settings.
  • Formula:
  • Recommended gram = (Required YAN - MeasuredYAN) * Liters * ppm per Gram Per Liter

Acid Additions

  • Run a bench trial to determine what mixture of acid is desired.
  • Formula:
  • Tartaric grams = ( Desired pH - Measured pH) * 10) * (Tartaric Percent/100) * Liters
  • Malic grams = ( Desired pH - Measured pH) * 10) * ( (Tartaric Percent/100) / 1.2 ) * Liters
