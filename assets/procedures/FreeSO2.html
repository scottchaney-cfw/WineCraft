Free SO2 Vacuum Aspiration (A/O) Method

Standardization of NaOH reagent
  • Boil at least 100mL of DI water.
  • Place the DI water container in an ice bath to bring to warm temp (25C).
  • Add 10mL of the DI water to a small beaker on a stir plate.
  • Add 4 drops of phenolphthalein to beaker.
  • Add 2mL of buffer 7 to act as a buffer for more accurate reading.
  • Using pipette, add .1N NaOH until around pH 7.5 then add .01N NaOH to get solution to 8.2 pH.
  • Using pipette, add 5mL of .01N HCl to beaker
  • Titrate with .01N NaOH back to 8.2pH
  • Record the amount of NaOH used in the field labelled Standardize .01N NaOH.
  • Once another field is touched the value for .01N NaOH Actual Normality will automatically update.

Running of Sample, with Pump

  • Using pipette, add 10mL of 3% hydrogen peroxide to the non-WS flask.
  • Add 6 drops of SO2 methyl-blue indicator to the non-WS flask.
  • Using pipette, add 40mL of DI water to the non-WS flask.
  • Using burette or pipette, add .01N NaOH drop by drop to the non-WS flask until grey-green color change.
  • Stopper the non-WS flask in preparation for a quick connection to the sample flask.
  • Using pipette, add 40mL of wine sample to WS flask.
  • Using pipette, add 10mL of 25% Phosphoric Acid to WS flask, and quickly apply the stopper.
  • Turn on pump apparatus for 10 minutes. You can use the timer on this app screen.
  • Using Burette, titrate .01N NaOH until grey-green color change. If the flask solution is already grey-green, not pink, then no free SO2 is present.
  • Record the amount of NaOH titrant required to reach color change in mL in the Amount titrant .01N NaOH field.

Running of Sample, with Vacuum

  • Fill impinger to fill line with 3% hydrogen peroxide.
  • Add 3 drops of SO2 methyl-blue indicator and close with cap.
  • Add 20mL of phosphoric acid to the bulb flask.
  • Cover with cap and remove sample addition bung.
  • Pipette 20mL of wine sample into bulb flask and close bung quickly.
  • Turn on vacuum apparatus for 10 minutes.
  • When bubbling stops, remove cap from impinger and titrate with NaOH via color change to grey/green.
  • Record the amount of NaOH titrant required to reach color change in mL.
