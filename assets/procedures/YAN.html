YAN Procedure

Formaldeyhde is toxic, titrate under an exhaust hood and handle with gloves.

Equipment

  • 10mL burette, filled with .1N NaOH
  • Calibrated pH meter with probe on stand
  • Formaldehyde 37% in sealed container, store away from light
  • 20mL pipette for formaldehyde
  • 50mL of clarified wine/juice sample
  • 50mL pipette for sample
  • Pipetter
  • 125mL beaker for adjusted wine sample
  • (2) 250mL beakers

Setup

  • Calibrate and rinse pH probe with DI water,dry probe.
  • Prepare 10mL burette with .1N NaOH under vent hood.
  • Position pH probe and stir plate under hood.

Sample Prep

  • Clarify sample with centrifuge or DE vacuum filter.
  • If SO2 was used, add 3 drops of H2O2.
  • Add approximately 30mL of .1N NaOH to the 125mL beaker.
  • place the pH probe into the beaker on the stir plate with a stir stone.
  • Dropper wine into the beaker until pH of 8.5 is reached.
  • Transfer sample to 250mL beaker.

Formaldehyde Prep

  • Cover and turn on range fan.
  • Check formaldeyde pH. Adjust to 8.5 with a dropper using .1N NaOH.
  • Re-check pH and adjust between tests as the pH will drift.
  • Add 20mL of 37% formaldehyde to the 250mL beaker with sample.
  • Re-seal formaldehyde container to limit exposure.

Titration

  • Stir sample with magnetic stirrer for 2 minutes. pH will begin to drop.
  • Using burette, titrate sample with .1N NaOH to end point of 8.5 pH.
  • Record mL of NaOH titrant used.

Math:

YAN mg/L = (mL of .1N NaOH used) * 28mg/L
