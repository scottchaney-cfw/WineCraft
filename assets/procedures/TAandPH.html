Total Acid and pH Procedure

pH Meter Calibration

  • Press On button.
  • Fill small beaker with 7 buffer.
  • Fill small beaker with 4 buffer.
  • Unscrew cap on probe cover, slide cap up probe.
  • Place probe and temp probe in 7 buffer. (Bring buffer to 25C if temp probe does not fit.)
  • Press Calibrate button.
  • When hour glass disappears and CFM is flashing press the CFM button, take probe out, rinse with DI water and shake.
  • Place probe in 4 buffer.
  • When hour glass disappears and CFM is flashing press the CFM button, take probe out, rinse with DI water and shake.

Press measure button to verify curve fit is 96 or higher.

Enter the normality of the .1N NaOH into the field .1N NaOH Actual Normality if the NaOH has been standardized with .1N HCl in the past 15 days. Otherwise follow the .1N NaOH Standardization Procedure.

.1N NaOH Standardization Procedure

  • Boil at least 100mL of DI water.
  • Place the DI water container in an ice bath to bring to warm temp (25C).
  • Add 100mL of the DI water to a beaker on a stir plate.
  • Add 4 drops of phenolphthalein to beaker.
  • Add 5mL of any wine (or buffer 7) to act as a buffer for more accurate reading.
  • Using pipette, add 1N NaOh until around pH 7.5 then add .1N NaOH to get solution to 8.2 pH.
  • Using pipette, add 5mL of .1N HCl to beaker
  • Titrate with .1N NaOH back to 8.2pH
  • Record the amount of NaOH used in the field labelled Standardize .1N NaOH.
  • Once another field is touched the value for .1N NaOH Actual Normality will automatically update.
  • Proceed to Subsequent TA Test.

Initial Setup for TA

  • Boil at least 100mL of DI water.
  • Place the DI water container in an ice bath to bring to warm temp (70F).
  • Add 100mL of the DI water to a beaker on a stir plate.
  • Add 4 drops of phenolphthalein to beaker.
  • Add 5mL of any wine to act as a buffer for more accurate reading.
  • Using pipette, add 1N NaOh until around pH 7.5 then add .1N NaOH to get solution to 8.2 pH.

Add drops of buffer 7 if overshot to come back to 8.2 pH.

Subsequent TA Test

Record pH
  • Warm wine sample to 25C
  • Rinse probe with DI water and shake.
  • Place probe in sample and move continuously.
  • Record pH reading when hourglass disappears.
  • Rinse probe with DI water and shake.
  • Adjust the volume of solution in the beaker back to 100mL.
  • Using wide mouth pipette, add 5mL of wine sample to beaker.
  • Titrate to 8.2 pH using .1N NaOH.
  • Record mL of NaOH into .1N NaOH Amount Used to get g/L TA.

To run the next TA test, repeat Subsequent TA Test
