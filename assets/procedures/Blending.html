Volume Concentration Calculations

Volume concentration calculations are one of the most common formulas used to blend and make additions during bench trials.

To use the quick calc feature, enter the base volume and concentration. The units used are not important, just be consistent so that if Liters are used for the base, they are also used for the addition or combined volume.

Enter the volume and concentration for the wine you have as the base. You can then enter volume and concentration for the addition (another wine, sugar solution, acid mixture, etc.). If you want to know how much of an addition to add you can enter the target volume and concentration instead and the addition will be calculated for you.

Blending Calculations

You can blend three wines at a time to get the final target wine value.

Enter the values for the volume for the first wine and then any of other items (Alc, VA, TSO2). The table can be used to calculate any value, so if you want to calculate RS, you can use the Alc field and it will calculate the concentration for you.

You may instead choose to select a wine you have already entered information on. Select the wine from the pulldown and the latest values for that wine will be populated into the correct fields.

You can blend more than three wines by performing one blend and then another. The percentages for each element will then need to be determined separately.
